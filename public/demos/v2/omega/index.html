    I

    Persona

    Ten questions. Not a blank system prompt.

    The Persona Builder interviews the expert in plain language — identity, role and primary use case, audience, communication style, advice style and guardrails. Personality scales for tone, energy, detail, humour and warmth. The answers become the twin's long-lived personalisation context, visible only to its administrators.

    II

    Instructions

    Optimism with evidence. Patience with a question. Precision with a claim.

    The interview drafts the twin's instructions — role, expertise, how it structures an answer, when it escalates to a human, what it must never do — and the expert edits them directly. Source prioritisation keeps answers grounded in her own material. With persona mode on, the twin stays fully in character, typed and spoken alike.

    III

    Voice

    A realtime voice. A cloned voice the expert holds the rights to. A face on screen.

    Convo Mode is spoken conversation in real time — no push-to-talk, no waiting for a page of text. A realtime voice provider is chosen with Praxis AI at scoping; cloned voices are supported where the customer holds the rights; an on-screen avatar layers lip-synced video on top of the voice. A per-twin pronunciation dictionary makes sure every name is said right.

    Expert approved · provenance verified

    03 Vault

    Your IP stays yours — protected in the patent-pending IP Vault™.

    Books, papers, talks and unpublished notes live in a private library only the expert controls. Every file is parsed, transcribed and indexed; every grounded answer carries a badge and numbered citations that open the original. Nothing is invented on the expert's behalf.

    • CollectionsNested folders with colour that follows the file through every breadcrumb.
    • RetentionUse renews the clock — only files that genuinely go unused ever expire; trash is restorable.
    • Citations requiredEach reply lists every passage that fed it: source file, matched text, relevance score.
    • Expert approvalNothing goes live without the expert's sign-off. Confidential files inform answers but never leak.

    04 Govern

    PraxisShield™ watches every word.

    Governed · live since day one

    The constitution · five rules, no exceptions

    1. §1Guardrail instructionsOne guardrail text applies to every conversation on the twin — typed and spoken, across every assistant. Where it conflicts, the guardrail wins.
    2. §2Threat monitoringConversations are screened in the background; incidents surface in the Admin Console with severity, status and a full triage history.
    3. §3Purpose-boundThe twin answers only within the mission it was built for, and says it does not know rather than improvising.
    4. §4Provenance-verifiedEach claim traces to a source inside the IP Vault™; the Histories log ties every answer to its user, model and retrieval sources.
    5. §5RevocableAny session can be revoked, any file trashed, any twin paused or retired — the expert stays in control.
