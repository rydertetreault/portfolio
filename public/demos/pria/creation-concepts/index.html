Personal

Personal Twin

A digital twin of yourself. Private by default — your knowledge, your voice, used by you, your team, or your clients. Joining defaults to disabled.

Community

Community Twin

A twin of a significant person — a mentor, creator, family member, or public figure. Others subscribe and chat with it. Optional free or paid subscription.

A

Express Wizard

3 steps · same wizard mental model

The safest evolution: keeps the familiar stepper, but collapses 8 steps into 3 — Identity, Personality (only the 5 required questions), Review & Create. Everything else becomes a smart default you can change later.

~2 min5 questionslowest risk
B

Conversational Create

chat-first · Pria interviews you

Creation as a conversation. Pria asks 7 quick questions in a chat, then presents a persona card to tweak and confirm. On-brand for an AI product — the twin is born the way it will be used.

~60 sec7 questionsmost on-brand
C

Single-Page Smart Form

no steps · live preview

One scrolling page with progressive disclosure and a live twin-profile preview that updates as you type — instructions auto-generate in real time. Power users create in one pass, nothing is hidden behind steps.

~90 sec5 required fieldsmost transparent
D

Template Gallery

pick · personalize · create

Near-instant creation: choose Personal or Community, pick a template that pre-answers 11 of 14 persona questions, personalize 2–3 fields, done. Blank Canvas still routes to the full flow for those who want it.

~30 sec3 fieldsfastest
E

Combined Flow ★ Recommended

D's gallery + B's chat + C's review page

The synthesis: pick a template or start fresh. Templates skip straight to a prefilled review page; starting fresh runs the 60-second chat with Pria, which then lands on the same filled-out page — where users can add optional detail before creating.
